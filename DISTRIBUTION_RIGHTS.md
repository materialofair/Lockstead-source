# Distribution rights requiring owner review

GPLv3 section 10 prohibits additional restrictions on recipients' GPL rights. Apple's distribution and usage terms need to be assessed against those obligations for a proposed App Store build. Historical FSF commentary identifies an App Store/GPL conflict; it is not a determination of this app's current legal position.

If the owner controls all relevant copyrights, separate permission or dual licensing may be possible at the owner's discretion. That permission cannot be invented for external contributions, SDKs or third-party resources. No Apple exception, dual license, third-party waiver or change to store terms is granted by this repository. Publishing source under GPL does not automatically resolve the distribution rights for existing App Store binaries.

References: https://www.gnu.org/licenses/gpl-3.0.en.html ; https://www.gnu.org/licenses/gpl-faq.en.html ; https://www.fsf.org/blogs/licensing/more-about-the-app-store-gpl-enforcement .

No specific incompatible third-party source dependency was identified in this snapshot. The owner has authorized GPL-3.0-only publication of their own source. The general distribution question does not establish that publishing this source repository is prohibited. This file does not give a legal conclusion on the existing App Store build.
